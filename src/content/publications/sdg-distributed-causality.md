## Abstract

Understanding the causal interconnectedness among the United Nations Sustainable Development Goals (SDGs) is essential for prioritizing policy interventions and preventing unintended trade-offs. This study introduces an empirical framework combining Panel Vector Autoregression (Panel VAR) with conditional independence analysis to evaluate distributed causality and directional relationships across multi-country SDG indicators.

## Key Highlights

- **Panel VAR Modeling**: Modeled dynamic endogenous feedback loops across multiple SDG dimensions while controlling for country-level heterogeneity.
- **Conditional Independence Analysis**: Applied statistical conditional independence tests to untangle direct versus mediated causal relationships across the development network.
- **Policy Trade-off & Synergy Insights**: Identified key systemic leverage points where targeted investments produce distributed synergies across interconnected sustainability targets.
