## Research Statement

My overarching research vision centers on **advancing trustworthy, data-efficient artificial intelligence and statistical learning methodologies** with high-impact applications in **Public Health, Epidemiology, and Low-Resource Natural Language Processing (NLP)**.

Coming from a strong foundation in **Mathematical Statistics**, my research philosophy emphasizes that modern machine learning models should not only deliver high predictive accuracy but also provide robust uncertainty quantification, generalizability, and empirical interpretability. I focus on developing statistical modeling frameworks and deep learning pipelines that extract reliable insights from high-dimensional, complex, and noisy data streams.

---

## Core Research Themes

### 1. Natural Language Processing & Multilingual NLP
- **Low-Resource & Code-Mixed Languages**: Developing robust text classification, sentiment analysis, and semantic understanding pipelines for low-resource languages (e.g., Bengali and multilingual code-mixed social datasets).
- **Multi-Model & Ensemble Architectures**: Designing hybrid systems that combine contextual transformer embeddings (BERT, RoBERTa, Banglish-BERT) with ensemble classifiers for improved robustness against noisy real-world data.
- **Aspect-Based Sentiment & Intent Classification**: Analyzing consumer, clinical, and civic sentiment to quantify public perception and automated feedback systems.

### 2. AI in Public Health & Epidemiological Modeling
- **Disease Surveillance & Biostatistics**: Utilizing statistical regression, survival analysis, and spatial-temporal modeling to track disease outbreaks, epidemiological trends, and public health risk factors.
- **Healthcare Data Analytics**: Developing predictive models for patient outcomes, maternal/child health indicators, and healthcare accessibility metrics in developing regions.
- **Evidence-Based Policy Insights**: Translating complex biostatistical models into actionable insights for healthcare policymakers and clinicians.

### 3. Statistical Machine Learning & Inference
- **Uncertainty Quantification & Bayesian Methods**: Applying probabilistic inference, Bayesian statistics, and bootstrap methods to measure confidence in deep learning predictions.
- **Multivariate Data Analysis & Feature Selection**: Developing principled statistical methods for dimensionality reduction, multi-collinearity management, and robust hypothesis testing in large-scale datasets.
- **Model Interpretability & Evaluation**: Establishing rigorous evaluation metrics beyond standard accuracy to prevent data leakage and distributional bias.
